Property Selling

Selling property in Istanbul at the right price and without legal loose ends: valuation, marketing, buyer screening and a clean title deed transfer, handled for you.

Contact us WhatsApp

What’s included

  • Valuation and strategy

    A realistic price based on recent sales and current demand in your district, and a plan for timing and presentation.

  • Marketing

    Professional photos, floor plans, listings on the main portals and our own buyer network, including international investors.

  • Buyer management

    Viewings, offer negotiation and checks on the buyer’s financing so you only deal with serious buyers.

  • Closing and transfer

    Sales contract, valuation report where required, tax and fee calculations, and the title deed transfer at the Land Registry, with or without you present.

How it works

  1. 1

    Assessment

    We visit the property, review the deed and any existing tenancy, and give you a price range with the reasoning behind it.

  2. 2

    On the market

    Preparation, photography and listing. You get a summary of inquiries and viewings every week.

  3. 3

    Sale

    We negotiate the best offer, prepare the paperwork and complete the transfer. The proceeds reach your account, in Türkiye or abroad.

Selling your property with TFC Türkiye

A sale is the moment an investment pays off, and small decisions have a large effect on the result: the asking price, how the apartment is presented, which buyers see it and how the transfer is handled. We manage the whole process so the property sells at a fair price and the paperwork is finished properly.

Pricing based on real sales

Overpriced listings sit on portals for months and then sell below their value. We set the price from recent comparable sales, the current stock in your district and the condition of the property, and we tell you what a fast sale and a patient sale would each realistically bring.

Presentation and reach

Clean, well-lit photography, a floor plan and an honest description do most of the work. Listings go on the portals buyers in Türkiye actually use, and properties that suit foreign investors are also offered to our own client base and partner agencies abroad. We handle viewings ourselves, at times that suit you or your tenant.

Selling a rented property

A property with a paying tenant can be attractive to investors and can be sold without ending the lease. We explain the tenant’s rights, prepare the notices the law requires and coordinate viewings so the tenancy is not disturbed.

Closing without loose ends

  • Verification of the buyer’s identity and funds before the contract is signed
  • Sales contract, valuation report and the calculation of title deed fees and taxes
  • Transfer at the Land Registry, by power of attorney if you are abroad
  • Settlement of utilities, site fees and the handover of keys and documents

Foreign owners often ask about tax. In general, gains on property held for more than five years are exempt from income tax for individuals; shorter holdings may be taxed. Our accountant checks your situation before the sale so there are no surprises afterwards, and the proceeds can be transferred abroad through your Turkish bank.
